# Animation
**length**: 2 to 3 minutes
**style**: Digital 2D, Mixed-Media

A time-line sequence, slow and tactile, showing the recording of knowledge and passing it on to following generations: Ancient Sumerian and Babylonian civilisations carved in stone and wrote on clay tablets in cuneiform. Greek phylosophy, plays and ancient science on papyrus; a stylised Library of Alexandria, filled with rows of manuscript roles; a medieval monk’s hand copying a manuscript slowly by candlelight; a rudimentary printing press; a 19th-century scientific notebook with round reading glasses, next to a microscope; technical textbooks and diagrams for modern science, etc.. Keep it quiet and physical — paper, ink, stone, dust. No glossy stock “cyber” imagery. Use calendars, globes, books, ancient cuneiform clay tablets and rock carvings, museum pieces, old instruments etc.
