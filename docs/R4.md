# r4 — Touch Display 2 + UPS HAT (B) + Rii K06

r4 is the build the **22 Oct 2026 order** describes. It replaces r2/r3's display, power board, cells and
keyboard, so unlike r3 it is a real geometry change. This page records the measured inputs, the decisions
they forced, and what to verify on your own parts before printing.

`cad/common.scad` holds one parameter set with two switches:

| Switch | Default | Selects |
|---|---|---|
| `R4` | `true` | r4 stack. `-D R4=false` restores r2/r3. |
| `R2` | `false` | r2/r3 mechanics. `-D R2=false` selects the original r1 case (and forces `R4` off). |

`pick(a, b, c)` gives the r4, r2/r3 or r1 value of a parameter.

```bash
python3 cad/tools/export.py            # r4 (default)
python3 cad/tools/export.py --r2       # r2/r3
python3 cad/tools/export.py --r1       # r1
openscad -D 'CHECK="bottom:ups"' cad/assembly.scad   # clearance check
```

## Decisions

**Split plane moved up.** r4's display is 14.92 mm deep, three times r2's 4.9 mm. Keeping the old rule
(`H_BOT = T - H_FACE`, 10.5 mm face) would cut the display in half. r4 sets `H_BOT = PI_PCB_TOP + 2.5`, so
the whole display body is in the face part and the Pi stays whole in the bottom shell. The TD2's own 8.5 mm
standoffs span the split; the printed case posts stop at the Pi pcb. `face_part()` is clipped at `H_BOT` so
no printed feature crosses the plane.

**The Pi is held by the case, not by the display.** The TD2's inner pattern is 51 × 49 mm; the Pi's is
58 × 49. Only the 49 mm axis matches.
- `DISP_HOLES` uses the TD2's **outer** standoffs (122.74 × 72.96) — the case posts meet the display's own
  M2.5 standoffs.
- `PI_HOLE_*` uses the Pi's **58 × 49** pattern, so the Pi screws to the case posts. The Pi is therefore
  offset 3.5 mm from the display's centre line along x; that is deliberate, not a rounding error.
- **Verify** the TD2 drawing dimensions against your unit before printing: if the outer standoffs differ,
  edit `DISP_HOLE_W/H/IX/IY`.

**No printed cell tray.** The HAT (B) has its own soldered 18650 holders, so `cell_tray()` is skipped in r4
(`if (!R4) cell_tray();`). The stock acrylic base and standoffs are not used. `CELL_*` still describes the
cells (for the stand-in and clearance): 2 cells with their axes along y, 19.3 mm apart across x.

**The HAT (B) is rotated 90°.** It has the same 85 × 56 outline as the Pi and the same 3.5 mm corner hole
offsets, so mounted in the same orientation its board would sit directly under the Pi's USB/Ethernet port
bodies. Those bodies are 13.6–16 mm tall on the component side, and the pogo gap is only 6 mm — the
interference check (`pi:ups`) reported a 10 × 51 × 9.5 mm collision. r2 avoided the same problem by ending
its 88 mm HAT (E) short of the port edge. r4 instead mounts the HAT at `UPS_W` 56 × `UPS_H` 85, which is
29 mm narrower than the Pi along x and puts the jack/USB-A/switch overhang past the Pi's +y end. **Do not
"fix" this by aligning `UPS_W/H` with the Pi** — that reintroduces the collision.

**Keyboard pocket crosses the split.** The K06 is 16.5 mm thick and the face part is only 14.42 mm, so the
pocket is formed by both printed parts:
- `face.scad` `kb_ribs()`: walls from the plate down to the split plane (it used to stop at the PCB).
- `keyboard_support.scad`: a shelf frame from `KBS_Z0` to 0.2 mm behind the keyboard body. In r4 it is one
  large opening (the body needs the volume), not the r2 two-window PCB frame.
- `KB_WIN` is a 146 × 81 mm opening in the 2 mm plate over the keys and touchpad. The touchpad is at the
  bottom of the K06, so the window cannot be a key-only opening.

**Charge input.** The HAT (B) charges from 8.4 V on a DC barrel jack. The r4 case therefore has a
**Ø9.5 mm round hole** with a 14 mm O-ring seat (`CHG_W`, `CHG_H`, `CHG_REC_W/H`) for a 5.5 × 2.1 mm panel
socket, not the r2 USB-C rectangle. The internal module (`MPPT_*`) must output **8.4 V**; the r2 bq25185
(5 V out) does not apply. Solar and the crank still share this one sealed port.

**Gauge.** The HAT (B) reports through an **INA219 at 0x42**, not the HAT (E)'s BQ4050 at 0x2D. The firmware
driver was replaced (`firmware/include/ups_hat_b.h`, `firmware/src/ups_hat_b.cpp`): config `0x399F`, 0.5 mA
current LSB, calibration 8192, pack voltage on the bus register. There is no per-cell data and no capacity
register, so the percentage is derived from the 2S pack voltage (6.0 V → 0 %, 8.4 V → 100 %) and the time
estimates from a 3200 mAh capacity constant. On-screen state names are `discharging` / `charging` / `full`.

**Status OLED band is tight.** The display ends at y = 97.46 and the K06 pocket starts at 133, so the band is
35.5 mm; the OLED module is 34 mm. `OLED_CY` is centred in that band. If your module's PCB is larger, the
window and the module clash — check before printing.

**Floor layout.** With 2 cells there is free floor under the keyboard. The DS3231 (`RTC_*`), the charger
module (`MPPT_*`) and the FireBeetle (`FB_*`) share it, clear of the four support columns (`KBS_POSTS`) and
of the sensor chamber. Changing one means re-running the `CHECK` set.

## Verify before printing

```bash
python3 cad/tools/export.py                     # expect bad edges: 0 on every part
openscad -D 'CHECK="bottom:kbs"' cad/assembly.scad    # and the other pairs in work/scripts/check.sh
```

1. **HAT (B) holder/cell side = 23.35 mm.** The single biggest depth risk. Measure board + cells + holders.
2. **TD2 standoffs 8.5 mm and outer pattern 122.74 × 72.96.** Also confirm the 14.92 mm overall depth.
3. **K06 outline 155 × 89 × 16.5.** If it differs, edit `KB_BODY`, `KB_PCB` and `KB_WIN` together.
4. **OLED module ≤ 34 mm** in y, and the PBS-33B nut clears the top wall.
5. **18650 length 65 mm** (≤ 67 mm per Waveshare), and **no 40-pin header** on the Pi (the UPS board sits there).
6. **NVMe mounting — unsolved.** The ordered **Seeed M.2 BASE + WD SN740 512 GB (2242)** is ≈10 mm tall
   with the card. The space under the Pi is the 6.5 mm UPS pogo gap and the floor holds the DS3231,
   charger module and FireBeetle, so there is no bay for it in the current geometry. Decide the
   location (and add `NVME_*` parameters) before exporting the bottom shell. The unstacking route that
   would free this space — and what it does and does not save in depth — is analysed in
   [`SLIMMING.md`](SLIMMING.md).
7. **DC socket** outer thread length vs the 3 mm wall + 9 mm pocket (the socket body pocket is 13 mm deep).
8. **Pi vs HAT (B) rotation:** keep the board at `UPS_W` 56 × `UPS_H` 85. Aligning it with the Pi's 85 × 56
   puts the board under the USB/Ethernet bodies and fails `pi:ups`.

## What r4 gives up versus r2/r3

| | r4 | r2/r3 | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pack energy | 2× 18650 ≈ 23 Wh | 4× 21700 ≈ 70 Wh | **≈2–2.5 h** vs ≈6–7 h of Pi 5 use |
| Charge path | 8.4 V DC, ≤2 A | USB-C PD, up to 40 W | Slower charging; solar/crank needs an 8.4 V module |
| Gauge detail | pack V/I/W (derived %) | per-cell V, %, remaining mAh, input state | Coarser battery warnings |
| Cooler | none (fits neither) | none | Pi 5 throttles under sustained load in both |
| Keyboard | K06 155 × 89, touchpad | Phistone 134 × 74 | Wider case (+22 mm); trackpad replaces the trackball |
