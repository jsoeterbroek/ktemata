- Iceberg green (Pantone 16-5808 TCX)

To match Pantone 16-5808 TCX (Iceberg Green)—a muted, soft sage/sea-green-gray with an approximate sRGB of (140, 156, 146)—using Vallejo acrylic paints, you need a desaturated base of green lightened with a heavy dose of neutral gray or off-white and balanced with a tiny drop of blue or yellow depending on the exact tint.

Suggested Vallejo Mixing Recipe

Because hobby paint formulas vary by pigment load, start with a major white/gray base and tint it gradually on a wet palette:

• Base Tone: Vallejo Model Color Pale Blue-Grey (70.905) or Medium Sea Grey (70.870) as your primary desaturating/lightening agent.
• Green Tint: Vallejo Model Color Camouflage Pale Green (70.971) or a tiny touch of Intermediate Green (70.891) / Reflective Green (70.890).
• Adjustment: Add micro-drops of White (70.951) if it is too dark, or a microscopic touch of Neutral Grey (70.993) to drop the saturation down to that characteristic textile-muted gray-green tone.
