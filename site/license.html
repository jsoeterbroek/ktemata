Why the split

Code wants to be reusable without strings attached: MIT is the shortest licence that lets you drop a driver or a script into your own project and not think about it again. A physical design is different — an STL or a case revision that drifts from its source is hard for the next person to fix. ShareAlike keeps improvements to the enclosure and the documentation open, which is the point of publishing a parametric case at all.

The short version. Take the firmware and the tooling, no obligations beyond the copyright line. Take the design, print it, sell the print — but credit it and keep your modified design under CC BY-SA 4.0 as well.
