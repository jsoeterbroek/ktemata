“A possession for all time”

A rugged offline-library cyberdeck with a very low power consumption: Raspberry Pi5 4Gb, a 5″ touch display, a 2S 18650 UPS and a compact keyboard in a sturdy case. It keeps Wikipedia, maps, manuals and books on the device — no network, no account, no cloud. Charged by solar and/or axial-flux hand crank if mains power is not available. Content is managed with Project NOMAD.

The name

Ktemata (κτήματα) is Greek for “possessions, things owned”. Thucydides opens his History of the Peloponnesian War by calling his work κτῆμα ἐς ἀεί, “a possession for all time”: written down so that what happened stays available to whoever comes later. That is the device’s job.

KISS

The whole project is based on the KISS principle - keep everything simple. The core is the Pi 5, the touch display, the keyboard, the UPS board with two 18650 cells, the SSD and the real-time clock. Everything else has a job and nothing more: the status OLED with its button reads out the pack with the Pi off and the Wi-Fi is a local access point with no uplink, off unless you switch it on. Charging is one sealed input for mains, solar or the hand crank. Extra boards, connections, adapters and switches are left out — each is another point of failure to debug when nothing else works.
