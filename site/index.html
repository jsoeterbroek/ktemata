A rugged offline-library handheld: Raspberry Pi 5, a 5″ touch display, a 2S 18650 UPS and a compact keyboard in a printed case. It keeps Wikipedia, maps and the Gutenberg books on the device — no network, no account, no cloud.

The name

Ktemata (κτήματα) is Greek for “possessions, things owned”. Thucydides opens his History of the Peloponnesian War by calling his work κτῆμα ἐς ἀεί, “a possession for all time”: written down so that what happened stays available to whoever comes later. That is the device’s job. The phrase is engraved on the case.

The build

Computer
Raspberry Pi 5
Display
Raspberry Pi Touch Display 2, 5″ (143.4 × 91.46 mm)
Power
Waveshare UPS HAT (B), 2× 18650 in 2S, 8.4 V DC charge input
Input
Rii K06 keyboard with touchpad (2.4 GHz dongle or Bluetooth)
Case
172 × 237 × 53 mm, parametric OpenSCAD prints
Charge
Solar or axial-flux hand crank generator

Everything for the device build — geometry, drawings, STLs, renders and notes — lives in the repository: github.com/jsoeterbroek/ktemata.

Everything for the compatible axial-flux hand crank generator build — geometry, drawings, STLs, renders and notes — lives in the repository: github.com/jsoeterbroek/handcrank.
