Build guide

A step-by-step record of the R6 cassette build, with photographs and short video of each stage. This page is a placeholder. The parts and the geometry are final enough to print; the photographs are being shot during the build itself.

Status: not yet written. Every step below is planned and will be filled in as the build progresses. Until then, the measurements, print settings and fit checks live in docs/R6.md, and the STLs in the repository.

Planned steps

Each heading becomes a section with its own photographs; the list is the running order, not the final wording.

01
Parts check — Pi 5, NVMe Base Duo and drives, UPS Module 3S with three 18650 cells, 7inch DSI LCD (C), K06, Peli 1200, pad foam
02
Print the cassette — tray, deck and lid plate; orientation and profile
03
Bolt the boards to the tray floor — the display/Pi/Duo stack and the UPS mounting posts
04
Connect the UPS Module 3S harness and the 12.6 V charge input
05
Fit the three cells, the PCH/FireBeetle and the DS3231
06
Glue the keyboard into the deck recess
07
Mount the display/Pi/Duo stack and the full-face deck
08
Route the DSI, touch and status-OLED leads through the deck slot and hinge side
09
Close the case and check the closed-case clearances
10
First boot, display rotation, hotspot, library install

Photographs

Image slots will be added per step as the build is photographed, using the same figure style as the Power page so captions and credits stay consistent.

Video

Short clips of the steps that are hard to photograph — soldering the power run, seating the display assembly, and the first closed-case fit — will be embedded here alongside the stills.

Everything on this page will be a record of one build, not a certified procedure. Lithium cells, mains chargers and soldered joints are your responsibility: check polarity and fusing before connecting a pack, and never charge unattended.
