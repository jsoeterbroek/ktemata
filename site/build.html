Build guide

A step-by-step record of the r5 cassette build, with photographs and short video of each stage. This page is a placeholder. The parts and the geometry are final enough to print; the photographs are being shot during the build itself.

Status: not yet written. Every step below is planned and will be filled in as the build progresses. Until then, the measurements, print settings and fit checks live in docs/R5.md, and the STLs in the repository.

Planned steps

Each heading becomes a section with its own photographs; the list is the running order, not the final wording.

01
Parts check — Pi 5, M.2 BASE and SSD, UPS HAT (B) with 2× 18650, Touch Display 2, K06, Peli 1200, pad foam
02
Print the cassette — tray, deck and lid plate; orientation and profile
03
Bolt the boards to the tray floor — the eight posts, and which screws go where
04
Solder the power run from the HAT to the Pi, in place of the pogo pins
05
Fit the cells, the FireBeetle, the DS3231 and the charger module
06
Glue the keyboard into the deck recess
07
Mount the display on the lid plate and tape it into the host lid
08
Route the FFC and GPIO leads through the hinge-side opening
09
Close the case and check the closed-case clearances
10
First boot, display rotation, hotspot, library install

Photographs

Image slots will be added per step as the build is photographed, using the same figure style as the Power page so captions and credits stay consistent.

Video

Short clips of the steps that are hard to photograph — soldering the power run, seating the display assembly, and the first closed-case fit — will be embedded here alongside the stills.

Everything on this page will be a record of one build, not a certified procedure. Lithium cells, mains chargers and soldered joints are your responsibility: check polarity and fusing before connecting a pack, and never charge unattended.
